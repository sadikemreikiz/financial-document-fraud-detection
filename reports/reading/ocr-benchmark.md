# OCR benchmark

> Generated by `make bench-ocr` (experiments/ocr_benchmark.py). Do not edit by hand.

Sample: 100 receipts drawn from the training split with seed 1. Reference: the dataset transcripts. Amount recall counts how many amounts of the form `123.45` in the transcript appear in the OCR output (multiset intersection per receipt). Word recall and precision use upper-cased tokens of at least two characters.
