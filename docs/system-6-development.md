# System 6 development

The development choices for system 6, TruFor
([ADR-0019](decisions/0019-system-6-trufor.md)), made on the design fold before its code is
frozen. The evidence is generated by
[`experiments/system6_development.py`](../experiments/system6_development.py) in
[system-6-development.md](../reports/forensics/system-6-development.md). TruFor runs on the
Apple GPU, whose outputs differ slightly from the CPU's, so that report is regenerated by
`make trufor` and not by `make reports`. Numbers on the design fold are optimistic; system 6 is
measured once on the clean folds, together with the other systems
([ADR-0020](decisions/0020-clean-folds-measured-together.md)).

TruFor is the work of F. Guillaro, D. Cozzolino, A. Sud, N. Dufour and L. Verdoliva
(CVPR 2023), used under its licence for nonprofit research.

## 1. Fixed before the run (2026-10-14)

Everything in this section was decided and committed before TruFor produced an output on any
receipt of the dataset. It rests on the smoke test of ADR-0019, which used synthetic pages, on
the sizes of the design-fold receipts and on TruFor's own code; no result entered it.

- **Code and weights.** `docfraud forensics download-trufor` downloads TruFor's repository at
  commit `ae54475` as an archive (124,143,535 bytes, SHA-256 `0ddef05c…`) and its weights
  (ADR-0019), verifies both against the pins in
  [`trufor.py`](../src/docfraud/forensics/trufor.py) and unpacks them into `artifacts/trufor`.
  The archive holds the same files as the copy inspected for ADR-0019. `uv run --with`
  supplies PyTorch 2.14.1, torchvision 0.29.1, timm 1.0.30 and yacs 0.1.8 for this run only;
  the project's environment stays without PyTorch.
- **Adapter.** The adapter builds the input as TruFor's loader does (the page on three
  channels, divided by 256) and reads the outputs as TruFor's test script does. On a synthetic
  page on the CPU, its map and detection logit are identical to those of TruFor's own loader.
  The checkpoint is loaded with `weights_only=True` (ADR-0019, decision 4).
- **Tiling.** A receipt of at most 1448 x 1448 pixels (2.1 MP) is analysed in one pass: 86 of
  the 113 design-fold receipts. No receipt lies between 2.03 and 2.17 MP, so the exact bound
  moves none. A larger receipt is cut into tiles of 1448 pixels per side that overlap by at
  least 128 pixels and start at multiples of 8; the last tile of a row or column starts at the
  last multiple of 8 from which it reaches the edge, and is up to 7 pixels longer. Starting on
  the 8 x 8 grid, every tile sees the receipt's JPEG block grid where the whole receipt has it.
  The 4 receipts between 2.1 and 3 MP take 2 or 3 tiles, the 23 page scans 20 or 24: 643
  passes in all. The largest possible tile, 1455 x 1455 pixels, took 4.1 s on a synthetic page.
- **Stitching.** Each pixel of the map comes from the tile in which it lies farthest from a
  tile edge, measured along each axis; the edge of the page is not a tile edge, since beyond it
  there is nothing a tile could miss. Overlaps are therefore cut in their middle.
- **Map.** System 6's map is TruFor's localisation map as it is, the probability that a pixel
  was manipulated. No variant is tried.
- **Document score, in a fixed order.**
  1. TruFor's integrity score, the sigmoid of its detection logit; for a tiled receipt, the
     highest over its tiles.
  2. If the size check of that score fails: the highest map value minus the receipt's own 99th
     percentile, the form chosen for system 1.
  3. If that fails too, the development round stops and the next step is discussed.

  The first score that passes is chosen, whatever its average precision, and both are reported.
  The size check is that of [ADR-0015](decisions/0015-pixel-measurement-rules.md) (section 6),
  on the authentic receipts with evidence
  ([ADR-0017](decisions/0017-size-check-on-evidence.md)), with the value over all 94 authentic
  receipts next to it. Page scans are the only receipts with many tiles, and the highest score
  over many tiles may grow with their number: this is what the check exists to catch.
- **Benign marks.** The report shows whether the top region of the map falls on a benign mark
  on the 58 flagged receipts, as the [benign marks guide](benign-marks-guide.md) fixes. The
  same table was due for system 1 and missing from its report; it has been added there as a
  description ([system 1 report](../reports/forensics/system-1-development.md)), and system 1
  stays frozen.

## 2. Results of the fixed order (2026-10-14)

The run of section 1, made from commit `f304150`, is in the development report as committed in
`a06d861`. In the fixed order, TruFor's integrity score, the highest over tiles, passed the size
check (+0.07, from −0.15 to +0.29) and came first; its average precision on the design fold was
0.278 (chance 0.168). The fallback failed the check (−0.35); its average precision was 0.249.
The top region of the map hit an edit on 6 of the 19 forged receipts.

## 3. Changed after the results: the integrity score of the whole receipt (2026-10-14)

**This section was written after the results of section 2 had been seen.**

- **What was found.** A check of where the scores of the page scans come from, made on the
  cached outputs, showed that on all 23 page scans the highest tile score came from a tile
  without print (fewer than 0.1 % of its pixels darker than 128), and that such tiles scored
  higher than the tiles that hold the receipt. On a page scan, the score of section 1 therefore
  measures how TruFor reacts to blank paper, not the receipt; the authentic page scans had a
  median score of 0.598, the receipts of one pass 0.379. The size check, which exists to catch
  a score that grows with the number of tiles, passed nevertheless: a rank correlation over 94
  receipts is a coarse test of an effect on the 20 largest. The development report now
  generates these counts (table "Where the highest tile score comes from"), and, since tiling
  was in question, how the maps behave at the cuts between tiles (table "The cuts between
  tiles"). The stitching itself places every value where it belongs, as its tests show.
- **Why the highest over tiles was the wrong reading.** ADR-0019 (decision 2) uses TruFor's
  integrity score. TruFor computes it from statistics pooled over every pixel of its maps (the
  soft minimum, soft maximum, mean and mean square of the confidence logits, and the same of
  the localisation logits weighted by the confidence) and passes them through its last layers.
  The highest score over tiles was this project's stand-in for receipts too large for one pass.
- **The change.** For a tiled receipt, the integrity score is now TruFor's last layers applied
  to the statistics pooled over the stitched maps: what TruFor would compute if the whole
  receipt fitted in one pass, except that each pixel's map values come from the context of its
  tile. A receipt of one pass keeps TruFor's own score, unchanged. No parameter is added. On a
  synthetic page, the pooled statistics reproduce TruFor's own logit within 1e-5 on the CPU;
  the report checks this on every receipt of one pass.
- **Rule, fixed before the new run.** The integrity score of the whole receipt replaces the
  outcome of section 2 if its size check passes, whatever its average precision; if it fails,
  the score of section 2 stays. All three scores are reported. The map is unchanged.
- **Safeguards**, as in [ADR-0017](decisions/0017-size-check-on-evidence.md): this section says
  that it was written after the results; every score tried stays in the report; numbers on the
  design fold are optimistic and are not presented as results; once its score is chosen,
  system 6 is frozen.

## 4. The score chosen (2026-10-14)

- **Chosen.** The run of section 3, made from commit `6d52fb5`, is in the
  [development report](../reports/forensics/system-6-development.md). The integrity score of
  the whole receipt passes the size check (−0.02, from −0.24 to +0.19) and is chosen. Its
  average precision on the design fold is 0.270 (chance 0.168), against 0.278 for the highest
  tile score it replaces: the change brought no higher number, and was not made for one.
- **Checks.** On the 86 receipts of one window, the pooled logit reproduces TruFor's own within
  1.4e-5. The maps and the tile logits of all 113 receipts are identical to those of the run of
  section 2 (compared on the cached outputs), so the map, its top regions and the mark checks did not change: the top region hits
  an edit on 6 of 19 forged receipts (mean random baseline 0.085) and falls on a benign mark on
  9 of 50 flagged authentic receipts (baseline 0.076); two authentic receipts with a digital
  note score 2nd and 10th highest among the 94.
- **Page scans.** The highest tile score came from a blank tile on all 23 page scans, 380 of
  whose 548 tiles are blank. With the whole-receipt score, the median of the authentic page scans
  falls from 0.598 to 0.471 (receipts of one pass: 0.379), and that of the 3 forged ones is 0.393:
  on the design fold, TruFor's score does not single out the forged page scans.
- **Cuts between tiles.** The maps of tiled receipts jump where tiles meet: across the cuts, the
  99th percentile of the differences between neighbouring pixels is 12 times that inside the
  tiles (median over the 23 page scans; at most 37), because each tile is seen in its own context
  and TruFor's map is smooth within a tile. The peak of the map lies within 8 px of a cut on 1 of
  the 27 tiled receipts. The map stays as section 1 fixed it; no blending of the overlaps was
  tried.

## System 6 is frozen

With its tiling, map and document score chosen, system 6 is frozen
([ADR-0017](decisions/0017-size-check-on-evidence.md)): no further variant is tried on the
design fold, and later changes are limited to defects (ADR-0014, decision 3), each reported with
both results. It is measured once on the clean folds, together with the other systems
([ADR-0020](decisions/0020-clean-folds-measured-together.md)).
