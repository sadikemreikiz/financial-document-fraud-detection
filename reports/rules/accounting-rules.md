# Accounting rules

Generated by `docfraud rules report` from run records; do not edit by hand.

The rules ([`docfraud.detectors.rules`](../../src/docfraud/detectors/rules.py)) check that a receipt's printed amounts add up. A logistic regression over their outcomes (fail or not applicable, per rule) gives the score. Development and measurement follow [ADR-0012](../../docs/decisions/0012-accounting-rules-evaluation.md): the rules were written by looking at training receipts, so results on the training split are optimistic, and the unbiased result is the single measurement on the validation split. Fields are read by parser v2 from the dataset transcripts (T1) or our OCR (T2).
