# 0029. How Phase 5 builds and measures copy-move, linking, fusion, the decision policy and the audit report

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-26 (before any Phase 5 component exists, before any fusion input of a
  validation receipt is computed and before any fusion model is fitted; earlier contacts with
  the data are listed under Disclosures)

## Context

Phase 5 completes the system of [ADR-0005](0005-evidence-based-architecture.md): copy-move
detection, linking of evidence across modalities, a calibrated fusion model, a decision policy
and an audit report, with the ablations that answer RQ2 to RQ4 of the
[project charter](../project-charter.md). Its exit criterion is an end-to-end system and an
ablation table.

Three records already fix part of it. Fusion is fitted on the training split, from inputs that
are out of fold wherever a component has fitted parameters, and the validation split serves
calibration and thresholds only ([ADR-0028](0028-fusion-fitted-on-the-training-split.md)).
Phase 5 is built and measured once, on the fields of the frozen LLM reader, which passed on both
text sources ([ADR-0024](0024-llm-extraction-before-fusion.md),
[ADR-0027](0027-llm-reader-judged-by-false-alarms.md)). The test split stays locked until
Phase 7 ([evaluation protocol](../evaluation-protocol.md), section 3).

The data are small: 94 forged receipts in the training split, 33 in the validation split and 35
in the test split. With so few, a choice made after seeing results, such as an input, a
comparison, a threshold or a group, can turn chance into a finding. Every such choice is
therefore fixed here, before the first fusion input exists. The numbers this record cites from
data are regenerated by `experiments/fusion_preregistration.py` in
[preregistration-data.md](../../reports/fusion/preregistration-data.md) and by
`experiments/copy_move_feasibility.py` in
[copy-move-feasibility.md](../../reports/fusion/copy-move-feasibility.md); numbers of earlier
phases are quoted from their reports.

## Options

Each decision below was taken separately; the options seriously considered were:

1. **Where Phase 5 is measured:** out of fold on the training split only; once on the
   validation split only; or both, the training split as the development reference and the
   validation split as one check of the frozen system.
2. **Pixel evidence:** every Phase 4 system; systems 2 and 1; system 2 only.
3. **Copy-move:** matching of near-exact duplicates without training; a learned detector; none,
   which would change the charter.
4. **Linking:** links from suspicious regions to read values and from those values to failing
   rules; only the second kind; both kinds with the exact inputs of each rule, which would
   change the frozen rules.
5. **The fusion model:** a logistic regression with default settings on 7 inputs; the same with
   one input per rule outcome (18 inputs); gradient boosting.
6. **RQ2's comparison:** fusion against every single source; against one source named now;
   against the source that is best on validation or test.
7. **The comparison for linking:** secondary; co-primary with a multiplicity correction; tested
   only if RQ2 holds; descriptive only.
8. **The ablation table:** single sources, each source left out and the score-level baseline;
   the full factorial of sources and links; only the comparators of the claims.
9. **RQ3:** additive contributions and catch counts per group; per-group drops in average
   precision when a source is left out; global coefficients only.
10. **The decision policy:** a review budget with two automatic outcomes; three bands with
    automatic rejection; a threshold from an assumed cost ratio; the max-F1 threshold.
11. **Where the audit report points:** the region of the finding that contributes most; any
    region the report shows; each detector's own region only.
12. **Track T3:** built now with a minimal file-metadata check; deferred to Phase 7; dropped.

## Decision

### 1. The training split is the development reference, the validation split one check

- Every Phase 5 result is computed out of fold on the training split (562 receipts, 94 forged)
  in the stored folds. It is the development reference and the source of the ablation table.
  It is labelled optimistic: components shaped by looking at training receipts keep that bias
  (ADR-0028, consequences), and section 7 (b) adds a smaller one. It is also reported without
  the design fold and without gold set A, the receipts on which the pixel systems, copy-move and
  the LLM reader's instruction were developed.
- When the system is frozen at a commit, the validation split (190 receipts, 33 forged) is
  scored once, as a check of the frozen system. Its threshold-free metrics (average precision,
  ROC-AUC, recall at a review budget) are unbiased under two conditions, which this record makes
  binding: calibration keeps the order of the scores (Platt scaling, not isotonic regression),
  and no fusion input uses a threshold chosen on the validation split. F1 at the threshold
  chosen there, Brier score and expected calibration error are labelled as measured on the
  receipts they were fitted on.
- Claims are made on the test split in Phase 7 by the rules of section 8. The validation check
  applies the same rules and is reported as a check.
- A rerun on the validation split is allowed only to fix a defect in the code, not because of
  the result; the defect, the fixing commit and both results are reported (as ADR-0014,
  decision 3).

### 2. Validation and test receipts are scored by components trained on the whole training split

As the evaluation protocol defines (section 4, step 2), every learned component that scores
validation and test receipts is trained on the whole training split: system 2 with seeds 0, 1
and 2 (about an hour per run on the reference machine in Phase 4), and the rules scorer of
Phase 3. Components without training (system 1, copy-move, the metadata check of section 13)
are computed directly. The fusion model, fitted on the out-of-fold inputs of the training
split, is applied to these inputs, which is standard stacking. These models are trained in
Phase 5, before the validation check, and Phase 7 reuses them.

### 3. Pixel evidence comes from systems 2 and 1

Fusion takes the document score and the top region of system 2 (synthetic forgeries,
[ADR-0022](0022-learned-models.md), frozen at `53194c4`) and of system 1 (the JPEG-lattice map,
[ADR-0016](0016-system-1-jpeg-trace.md)). In the clean measurement their average precision was
0.647 and 0.507 at a chance level of 0.167
([clean measurement](../../reports/forensics/clean-measurement.md)). Systems 3 and 4 share
system 2's architecture and were weaker (0.298 and 0.437); system 6, TruFor, was close to
chance (0.183); system 5 is a diagnostic, not a detector.

### 4. Copy-move matches near-exact duplicates, is developed on the design fold and measured once

- **Method.** No training. The detector looks for windows of about one character that are
  near-exact duplicates of another window on the same receipt. The receipts are lossless PNG
  files (all 562 of the training split), so a copy pasted inside a receipt keeps the grey
  levels, scan noise included, of its source, while
  a character printed twice differs by noise. Standard copy-move detectors (keypoints, block
  features) are built to match despite noise, rotation and scaling and would fire on every
  repeated character; a learned detector would repeat what system 2 learned from the same
  synthetic engine.
- **Feasibility.** On the design fold, 9 of the 14 annotated copy sources have an exact copy
  and 3 more a copy with at least 86 % of the ink pixels equal, while the closest natural twin
  of a character window on an authentic receipt reaches at most 34 %
  ([copy-move-feasibility.md](../../reports/fusion/copy-move-feasibility.md)). The check used
  the annotated sources; the detector searches all pairs, which gives chance twins more
  opportunities.
- **Development** uses the design fold and synthetic copy-paste forgeries of the engine
  ([ADR-0021](0021-synthetic-forgery-engine.md)) made from design-fold receipts only; the
  viewing lock of ADR-0014 holds. The detector reports a document score, the region of the
  pasted copy (its target) and the region it was copied from.
- **Measurement.** When development ends, the detector is frozen at a commit and measured once
  on the clean folds as a system of its own, by the rules of Phase 4: document average
  precision, the localisation guard of its target region
  ([ADR-0014](0014-pixel-forensics-preregistration.md), decision 4;
  [ADR-0015](0015-pixel-measurement-rules.md)) and the hit rate of its source region against
  annotated copy sources (evaluation protocol, section 5). CPI appears in 73 of the 94 forged
  training receipts, 59 of them in the clean folds, 46 of these with an annotated source. Only
  then does copy-move become a fusion input; it is not changed afterwards.

### 5. Linking: two kinds of link, without thresholds

Each receipt gets two binary inputs:

1. **A suspicious region lies on a read value**: the top region of system 2, the top region of
   system 1 when its map has evidence, or a copy-move target shares at least one pixel with the
   box of a value the frozen LLM reader read: an amount, a part of an item or a date.
2. **That value is read by a failing accounting rule.** Which field roles a rule reads comes
   from a fixed map from each rule to its roles, kept in the linking layer; the rules of
   [ADR-0012](0012-accounting-rules-evaluation.md) stay unchanged.

Regions are the top regions of ADR-0015, section 2, a rule without parameters. The pixel
thresholds chosen on the validation split (ADR-0015, section 5) are never used, so that the
validation check stays unbiased (section 1). A map without evidence has the whole page as its
top region and yields no link. The reader gives a box for 12,461 of the 12,589 values it read
on training transcripts and for every value on OCR text; a box is the segment or line a value
is printed in, and on authentic receipts the boxes of amounts and items cover a median 2.9 %
of the page.

### 6. The fusion model is a logistic regression with default settings on 7 inputs

- **Model.** scikit-learn's `LogisticRegression` with its defaults (L2 penalty, C = 1); nothing
  is tuned. Inputs are standardised inside each fold, on that fold's training part.
- **Inputs**, per text source:
  1. the accounting rules: the score of the Phase 3 scorer (a logistic regression over the
     fail and not-applicable outcomes of the six rules, 12 features), out of fold on the
     training split and fitted on the whole training split for validation and test;
  2. system 2's document score;
  3. system 1's document score and
  4. whether its map has evidence, since system 1 scores 0 without evidence
     ([ADR-0017](0017-size-check-on-evidence.md));
  5. copy-move's document score;
  6. link 1 and
  7. link 2 of section 5.
- **Score-level baseline** (ADR-0005, step 4): the same model and pipeline without the two link
  inputs, so that the difference between the two isolates linking.
- **One model per track.** T1 and T2 each get their own; T3 is section 13.
- **Calibration and threshold.** Platt scaling fitted on the validation split; the operating
  threshold maximises F1 on the validation split, ties going to the higher threshold
  (evaluation protocol, section 5).
- **Seeds.** Headline numbers use system 2's seed 0. The fusion model is also fitted with the
  inputs of seeds 1 and 2, and the spread is reported (evaluation protocol, section 6).

Why this model: by the sample-size criteria of Riley et al. (2020, BMJ 368:m441), at the
explained variation of system 2 alone out of fold (Cox-Snell R² 0.189, the lowest of its three
seeds), 7 inputs need 298 receipts and 5 need 215, against 562 in the training split, with an
expected shrinkage of 0.944 for 7 inputs. One input per rule outcome (18 inputs) would need 765
and is ruled out. Gradient boosting needs tuning and far more data for the same stability
(van der Ploeg et al., 2014, BMC Med Res Methodol 14:137); the links already make the
interactions between modalities explicit.

The rules input was chosen before its numbers on the LLM's fields were computed. Out of fold,
the Phase 3 scorer is at chance on parser v2's fields (ROC-AUC 0.498 on transcripts, 0.514 on
OCR text), as Phase 3 found, and informative on the frozen LLM reader's fields (0.645 and
0.609). The two simpler inputs in the data report, whether any rule fails and the number of
failing rules, were computed for comparison and are not used.

### 7. Safeguards against optimistic or misleading results

- **(a) Overfitting check.** The calibration slope of the fusion model's out-of-fold
  probabilities on the training split is reported. The expected value is about 0.94 (the
  expected shrinkage of section 6); a slope below 0.9 is reported as overfitting.
- **(b) Second-order optimism of the training results.** A component that scored fold *k* out
  of fold was trained on the other folds, including fold *j*; the fusion model that scores fold
  *j* is fitted on fold *k*'s inputs, so fold *j*'s labels reach it indirectly. The effect is
  expected to be small; it is not measured and is disclosed with every training result. The
  validation split is not affected.
- **(c) Calibration on the validation split.** Platt scaling is fitted there, so Brier score
  and expected calibration error on validation are labelled optimistic; they are unbiased only
  on the test split.
- **(d) Same inputs on both sides.** Training inputs come from the cross-validation models,
  validation inputs from the models trained on the whole training split (section 2). Before
  the validation check, the distribution of every input is compared between the two, without
  labels, and any shift is reported.
- **(e) Guard tests.** Tests check that fusion and linking inputs are computed from detector
  outputs and read fields only, never from labels or region annotations, and that the models
  of T1 and T2 never receive the metadata input of section 13.

### 8. Claims: RQ2 and linking are the only tests

- **RQ2** ("does fusion outperform the best single source?") is claimed for a track only if
  the fusion model beats **every** single source: the accounting rules alone, pixel evidence
  (systems 2 and 1 together) and copy-move alone. Each comparator goes through the same
  pipeline: a logistic regression on its inputs, out of fold on the training split, fitted on
  the whole training split for validation and test, and Platt scaling on validation. Each
  difference is the paired bootstrap of document average precision of the evaluation protocol
  (section 6), and all three 95 % intervals must exclude zero. This is an intersection-union
  test (Berger, 1982, Technometrics 24:295), which needs no multiplicity correction; it avoids
  naming one source now that could turn out not to be the strongest, and choosing the best
  source on the evaluation set, which would select on it.
- **The primary metric is document average precision**, as for Phase 4
  (ADR-0014, decision 4): F1 adds the noise of a threshold chosen on 33 forged receipts, and
  recall at a 10 % budget uses one point of the ranking. The success criterion of Phase 7
  ([ADR-0013](0013-success-criterion-and-floors.md)), based on F1 and recall at the 10 %
  budget, is not changed.
- **Linking** is a pre-named secondary comparison: the fusion model against the score-level
  baseline, by the same test, claimed only if the interval excludes zero, labelled secondary
  and reported whatever RQ2 gives.
- **T1 and T2** are both headline results (evaluation protocol, section 2), so there are four
  tests per track and eight in all. As in Phase 4, no correction for multiplicity is made and
  the number of tests is stated.
- **Power.** With about 33 forged receipts a difference must be roughly 0.14 in average
  precision to be found: in the clean measurement the interval of the RQ5 difference was 0.186
  wide on 75 forged receipts, and √(75/33) ≈ 1.5 times that is about 0.28. This is an estimate.
  RQ2 may well be inconclusive; it is then reported as such, with the same prominence
  (charter, success criteria).

### 9. The ablation table

Per track, the rows are: the floors (marking every receipt as forged; chance), the rules alone,
pixel evidence, system 2 alone, system 1 alone, copy-move alone, the fusion model without each
source in turn, the score-level baseline and the full fusion model. Leaving a source out removes
the source and every link derived from it, and the links are recomputed from the remaining
sources: without the rules there is no link 2; without pixel evidence the links come from
copy-move targets only; without copy-move, from the pixel regions only. The columns are average
precision with its 95 % interval (primary), ROC-AUC, recall at the 10 % and 20 % budgets, F1,
Brier score and expected calibration error. On the training split, F1 uses the threshold that
maximises F1 on the same out-of-fold scores and the calibration metrics use the model's own
probabilities, before Platt scaling; both are labelled optimistic. On the validation split, F1
uses the threshold chosen there and the calibration metrics are labelled as in section 7 (c).
The table is made out of fold on the training split and once on the validation
split, with seed 0 and the spread over seeds 0 to 2. Only the comparisons of section 8 are
claims; every other row is descriptive. The full factorial of sources and links (about 14 rows
per track) was rejected because more rows only offer more noise to read as findings.

### 10. RQ3: which evidence drives the decision

RQ3 is answered descriptively, by two views of every group, each count written as *x* of *n*
with the group's size:

1. **Contributions.** The fusion model is linear in its standardised inputs, so each receipt's
   log-odds split exactly into one contribution per input, the coefficient times the
   standardised value (for a linear model the same as SHAP values; Lundberg and Lee, 2017).
   Per group: the mean contribution of each input and how often each input contributes most.
   Platt scaling multiplies every contribution by the same factor, so their order is unchanged.
2. **Catches.** How many of a group's forged receipts each single source and the full fusion
   model find at the 10 % budget.

The groups are the forgery technique, the modified entity and the forged receipts that change
only what the accounting rules cannot check, plus the breakdowns of the evaluation protocol
(section 7): false alarms on authentic receipts with benign handwritten or digital marks, and
results by editing software. Groups are small, for example 26 CPI, 6 CUT, 3 PIX, 1 IMI and 1
CPO among the 33 forged validation receipts, so no group is tested. On the training split a
receipt's contributions come from the fusion model of its own fold; on the validation split,
from the model fitted on the whole training split. Inputs that move together, such as a link
and the score it derives from, share credit between them; this is stated with every table. A
per-group drop in average precision when a source is left out was rejected: on groups of one
to three forged receipts it carries no information.

### 11. The decision policy: review or accept, by a review budget

- **Two automatic outcomes.** A receipt is sent to **review** if its calibrated fusion
  probability is at least *t10*, otherwise it is **accepted**. *t10* is the 19th highest
  calibrated probability of the 190 validation receipts, so that 10 % of them would be
  reviewed; finding it uses no label. It is applied unchanged to the test split. *t20* (the
  38th highest, 20 %) is reported as an alternative setting.
- **No automatic rejection.** Rejecting a receipt, which means accusing someone of fraud, is the
  reviewer's decision after reading the audit report and, from Phase 6b, the investigation
  agent's case file ([ADR-0006](0006-llm-placement.md)). Out of fold on the training split,
  even the 5 % of receipts that system 2 ranks highest hold 3 to 6 authentic ones depending on
  the seed, and a band narrow enough to be safe would hold about four validation receipts,
  too few to estimate its precision. This amends step 5 of ADR-0005, which names accept,
  review and reject.
- **Why a budget.** The success criterion of Phase 7 is recall at the 10 % budget, so the
  policy uses the operating point that is already pre-registered, and the investigation agent
  of Phase 6b runs on about one receipt in ten. No cost data exist from which a cost ratio
  could be derived, and the max-F1 threshold, which flags about as many receipts as are forged,
  optimises F1 rather than a reviewer's capacity.
- **What is reported**, on the validation split and in Phase 7 on the test split, with
  intervals: the share of receipts sent to review, recall, and the precision of the reviewed
  receipts. Recall at the 10 % and 20 % budgets ranked on the evaluated split and F1 at the
  max-F1 threshold stay the metrics of the evaluation protocol.

### 12. The audit report, and whether it points at the forgery

Fixed by earlier records: the report is generated by a fixed template, without an LLM
(ADR-0006: LLMs read fields and investigate receipts sent to review, nothing else); every
receipt gets one ([architecture](../architecture.md)); it states only what detectors found
(ADR-0005, step 6; charter, principles); and its grounding rate is measured with a target of
100 % and every violation listed (evaluation protocol, section 5). A template makes the rate
100 % by construction; an independent checker, which reads the facts back from the rendered
report and looks each up in the findings, tests it anyway.

The report shows the probability, the decision of section 11, and the findings ordered by
their contribution (section 10), each with its reason, its values and its location, linked
findings together and the receipt with their boxes; a machine-readable record carries the same.

RQ4 for the report is measured as follows:

- The report's **first region** is the location of the finding with the largest positive
  contribution among those that have a location. A finding located by several boxes, such as a
  failing rule that reads several values, is located by their bounding box.
- It **hits** if it shares at least one pixel with an annotated modified rectangle or copy
  source (ADR-0015, section 3). The **random baseline** places a box of the same size on the
  same receipt, centred on its OCR text, as ADR-0015 defines. The report **points at
  forgeries** if the lower bound of the 95 % interval of its hit rate exceeds the baseline;
  otherwise the result is reported as a probable shortcut, as in Phase 4.
- A report without a located finding of positive contribution counts as a miss, and the number
  of such reports is stated.
- It is measured on every forged receipt with region annotations, out of fold on the training
  split and on the validation split, and also by technique, by entity and among the receipts
  sent to review, as *x* of *n*. For comparison, system 2's top region hit 0.689 of the clean
  forged receipts against a baseline of 0.034 in Phase 4.

A single first region is measured because Phase 4 measured each system by one top region, and
because "look here first" is what a reviewer needs; counting a hit for any region a report
shows would reward reports that show many.

### 13. Track T3 is built in Phase 5, with a minimal metadata check

The evaluation protocol defines track T3 as T2 plus the file-metadata detector, which
[ADR-0003](0003-content-only-headline.md) requires to exist and to be reported separately. No
such detector has been built, and the architecture assigns it to Phase 3 or 5.

- **The check.** Without training, it reads the file header and outputs 1 if the file carries
  any of five traits an editing program leaves: an alpha channel, an ICC colour profile, EXIF
  data, XMP data or a creation date. The list was fixed from the headers of the training split,
  where 88 of the 94 forged receipts and 1 of the 468 authentic ones carry at least one trait.
  Two other properties of the headers (a missing gamma chunk, a resolution chunk) are not
  traits of editing and are left out, as are the colour mode and the file size, which also
  differ between the classes ([data audit](../../reports/data/findit2-audit.md)): authentic
  scans are stored in colour too, and file size reflects compression as well as editing.
- **One binary input**, not the number of traits: the number would change the ranking of 7
  receipts only, and choosing it would fit the list to one authentic receipt.
- **Fusion.** T3 has its own fusion model, the T2 model with the check as an 8th input
  (Riley: 340 receipts needed), through the same pipeline.
- **Reporting.** T3 is reported apart from the headline, with ADR-0003's caveat that file
  traits disappear when a file is saved again, next to the check alone and the T2 fusion model.
  It enters no claim. Track R of the protocol is defined on T2 and does not measure T3; that
  re-saving removes the traits holds by construction.

### 14. Order of work

1. Copy-move: developed on the design fold, frozen, measured once on the clean folds.
2. Linking, fusion, the metadata check, the decision policy and the audit report, with the
   tests of section 7 (e).
3. Inputs of the training split, out of fold; system 1 on the design fold, which Phase 4 did
   not measure; system 2 and the rules scorer trained on the whole training split.
4. The tables of sections 8 to 13 out of fold on the training split.
5. The system is frozen at a commit; the check of section 7 (d); the validation check, once.
6. Phase 7 as the evaluation protocol defines.

## Disclosures

Contacts with the data before this record, beyond those listed by ADR-0014:

- **Training split, out of fold.** To decide sections 6, 11 and 13, the following were
  computed and looked at: system 2 alone (its strength and its operating points), the three
  ways of turning the rules into one input on parser v2's and the LLM's fields, how often
  annotated edits lie on located values, and the traits in the file headers. All are in the
  data report. No fusion input combining sources, no fusion model, no score of pixel evidence
  as a pair and no copy-move score has been computed.
- **Design fold.** The copy-move feasibility check opened its receipts, which ADR-0014 allows.
- **Validation split.** Its labels and region annotations were counted for the group sizes of
  section 10. Its receipts were read once by the frozen LLM reader in Phase 5's first step; the
  command showed no field, label or score ([LLM record](../llm-development.md)), and no rule
  outcome or score of a validation receipt has been computed.
- **Test split.** Nothing beyond the disclosures of the evaluation protocol.

## Consequences

- RQ2 and the value of linking get a fixed test, and every other number of Phase 5 is labelled
  descriptive, so that a result on 33 forged receipts cannot be selected after the fact. RQ2
  may well be inconclusive.
- ADR-0005's status line and the architecture's decision policy now say that a receipt is
  accepted or sent to review.
- Copy-move relies on lossless files. Track R of Phase 7, which re-encodes the test images as
  JPEG, will show how much of it survives.
