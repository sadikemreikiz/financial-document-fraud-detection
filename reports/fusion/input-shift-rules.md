# Where the rules' shift between the splits comes from

> Generated by `experiments/input_shift_rules.py`. Do not edit by hand.

A diagnostic of the [comparison of the inputs](input-shift.md), made after it and not pre-registered; nothing in the pipeline changes with its result. The rules' score of a training receipt comes from the scorer of its fold, one of five, and that of a validation receipt from one scorer fitted on every training receipt ([ADR-0029](../../docs/decisions/0029-fusion-preregistration.md), section 2). Applying the one scorer to the training receipts too shows whether the shift lies in the scoring or in the receipts. No label of a validation receipt is read.
