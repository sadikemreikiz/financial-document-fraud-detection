# How to compare the fusion inputs of the two splits

> Generated by `experiments/input_shift_method.py`. Do not edit by hand.

The evidence behind the method of the input comparison of [ADR-0029](../../docs/decisions/0029-fusion-preregistration.md), section 7 (d), chosen before any validation input was computed. Only the out-of-fold inputs of the training split are used, without labels: random splits into 372 and 190 receipts have no shift by construction, and moving one input of the smaller part makes a known one. The KS distance with a permutation p-value was chosen: where values tie, the asymptotic p-value finds a shift less often than the 5 % it should when there is none, and so misses real ones too; and the classifier finds a shift of one input less often than the KS distance does.
