# Copy-move feasibility on the design fold

> Generated by `experiments/copy_move_feasibility.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Whether a copy pasted inside a receipt is a near-exact duplicate of its source while natural look-alikes are not, checked before copy-move is pre-registered in [ADR-0029](../../docs/decisions/0029-fusion-preregistration.md). Only receipts of the design fold are opened. The check uses the annotated source boxes, which a detector does not have: a detector searches all pairs of windows, and more comparisons give chance twins more opportunities.
