A region stands out when its value lies below the 2.5th or above the 97.5th percentile of its 200 controls, which happens to 5 % of regions by chance. The probability is a one-sided exact binomial test; regions of one receipt are not independent, so it is a guide, not a test. Uninformative: compared regions whose value equals that of every control, for example a noise level of zero on paper clipped to pure white; they count as compared and cannot stand out. This column was added after the first run, which showed such cases; the plan had not foreseen them.
