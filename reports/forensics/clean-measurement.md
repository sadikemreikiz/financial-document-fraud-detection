# Clean measurement: the Phase 4 systems on the clean folds

> Generated by `experiments/clean_measurement.py` (`make clean-measurement`). Do not edit by hand. Not part of `make reports`: the systems run on the Apple GPU.

The single measurement of every Phase 4 system on the clean folds ([ADR-0020](../../docs/decisions/0020-clean-folds-measured-together.md)), recorded in [clean-measurement.md](../../docs/clean-measurement.md): 449 receipts of folds 0, 1, 3, 4, 75 of them forged. No result of a system was looked at before every system had run, and nothing below chose anything.

- Systems 2 to 4 trained at commit `2a8998f`; every system measured at commit `2a8998f`.

- RQ5: system 4 against system 3: average precision +0.139 (+0.056 to +0.242), claimed.
- Secondary: system 2 against system 3: average precision +0.348 (+0.243 to +0.443), claimed.
- System 1, JPEG lattice: detects forgeries by the localisation guard.
- System 2, synthetic forgeries: detects forgeries by the localisation guard.
- System 3, real forgeries: detects forgeries by the localisation guard.
- System 4, both: detects forgeries by the localisation guard.
- System 6, TruFor: detects forgeries by the localisation guard.
- The global-trace probe: the dataset shows no global trace (ADR-0018).

Stated with the results, as the records require:

- Conventions that no data chose (ADR-0022): the learning rate and its schedule, the number of steps, the shares of tiles, the 50 % mix of system 4 and the factor of the failure rule. Another mix or length could change the answer to RQ5; no search for the best one was made.
- Limitations of systems 2 to 4 (ADR-0022): system 4 sees each real forgery half as often as system 3; rectangles teach the unchanged paper inside them as forged; large rectangles stay as annotated; on page scans a tile spans about 11 characters, against about 25 on other receipts; training on the Apple GPU is not repeatable bit for bit. Their document score was added after the development round's results (ADR-0023), and system 2 kept part of its page-scan effect at the scale of the text on the design fold.
- System 1 sees only receipts that kept a JPEG lattice, and no page scan (ADR-0016); its trace is the most exposed to re-encoding, which the robustness track of Phase 7 measures.
- TruFor (system 6) is for informational and nonprofit use; credit: F. Guillaro, D. Cozzolino, A. Sud, N. Dufour and L. Verdoliva, TruFor: Leveraging All-Round Clues for Trustworthy Image Forgery Detection and Localization, CVPR 2023, pp. 20606-20615. The ImageNet terms limit systems 2 to 4 to non-commercial research.
