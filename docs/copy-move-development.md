# Copy-move development

The development choices for copy-move, the detector of copy-paste inside a receipt of
[ADR-0029](decisions/0029-fusion-preregistration.md) (section 4) and
[ADR-0030](decisions/0030-copy-move-reports-a-pair.md), made on the design fold before its code
is frozen. The evidence is regenerated by
[`experiments/copy_move_development.py`](../experiments/copy_move_development.py) in
[copy-move-development.md](../reports/fusion/copy-move-development.md); the feasibility check
that preceded ADR-0029 is in [copy-move-feasibility.md](../reports/fusion/copy-move-feasibility.md).
Numbers on the design fold are optimistic: every candidate was tried on its 14 receipts with
copy-paste inside. Copy-move is measured once on the clean folds.

## 1. The search (2026-10-26)

- **Exact duplicates, found by hashing.** Every square block of the page is hashed at every
  position, and equal hashes at positions at least one block apart are duplicates (the
  exact-match search of Fridrich, Soukal and Lukáš, 2003), each confirmed pixel by pixel
  (section 7). Receipts are lossless PNG files, so
  a copy pasted as a rectangle keeps its source's grey levels exactly, scan noise included; the
  feasibility check found 9 of 14 annotated copies exact and 3 more nearly so, while natural
  twins of a character on authentic receipts share at most 34 % of their ink pixels. A copy
  that is exact only in part still holds exact blocks inside it.
- **Block side: a quarter of the text height** (the median height of the receipt's OCR words,
  as for system 2, [ADR-0023](decisions/0023-learned-score-at-text-scale.md)), at least 4 px,
  so that a pasted character holds many blocks.
  A third and a fifth of the text height give the same average precision (0.918 and 0.921
  against 0.921); a fifth fails the size check. The quarter, the first value tried, stays.
- **Pairs.** Matches are grouped by their offset, the shift from one copy to the other, and
  each offset's matches into connected groups; the largest group of any offset is the
  receipt's pair. A hash shared by more than 16 positions is a repeated structure and is
  skipped.
- **Document score: the duplicated area in squared text heights**, about the number of
  characters copied, so that it does not grow with the scale of the scan; 0 without a pair.
- **Speed.** A receipt takes about 0.1 s (median; at most about 4.5 s) on the reference
  machine, as printed by the development script.
- Code: [`copy_move.py`](../src/docfraud/detectors/copy_move.py).

## 2. Which blocks count (2026-10-26)

Most blocks of a receipt are equal to others by nature: blank paper clipped to white, flat
tones, the texture of compressed paper. A block counts only if it could not repeat by chance.

- **Round 1: blocks that span ink** (darkest and lightest pixel at least 60 grey levels apart,
  the rule of the feasibility check). It found the pair on 12 of the 14 receipts with
  copy-paste inside, but **failed the size check** of
  [ADR-0015](decisions/0015-pixel-measurement-rules.md) (section 6): Spearman 0.612 (0.434 to
  0.748) over the 94 authentic receipts. On the page scans of 7016 × 4961 px, single specks of
  dust on paper clipped to white make identical blocks, and a larger scan holds more specks.
- **Round 2: blocks with unsaturated pixels**, at least a quarter strictly between 0 and 255,
  and not flat, without the ink rule. It was tried because one copy, on X51007339158, is a
  patch of blank paper that covers the old characters, which an ink rule cannot see. Average
  precision fell from 0.894 to 0.557 and the highest authentic score rose from 0.245 to 3.338:
  compressed paper repeats exactly at offsets that are multiples of 8 px (24 px across, or 8
  down and 16 across), the grid of JPEG compression. The paper copy was still not found.
- **Chosen: both rules.** Specks are saturated and fail the second rule; paper texture fails
  the first. Average precision 0.921 for receipts with copy-paste inside against authentic ones
  (0.769 for all forged receipts; chance 0.130 and 0.168); the pair hits on 12 of 14; the size
  check passes over all authentic receipts (0.034, from −0.155 to 0.222) and over the 25 with a
  pair (−0.032, from −0.486 to 0.411), as [ADR-0017](decisions/0017-size-check-on-evidence.md)
  runs it.
- **Benign marks.** Of the 50 flagged authentic receipts, 11 have a pair and on 1 of them
  (X51005453804, the 8th highest authentic score) a region lies on a mark, where random boxes
  of the same sizes would overlap one 13.9 % of the time on average
  ([benign marks guide](benign-marks-guide.md)).

## 3. A pair, not a copy and its source (2026-10-26)

Of the 12 pairs that hit, 2 have both regions on annotated copies. On the other 10, a stronger
break in the grey levels across a region's border picked the copy 7 times, and the copy was the
lower region 8 times. Neither is a rule one can trust, so copy-move reports the pair without
naming the copy, and [ADR-0030](decisions/0030-copy-move-reports-a-pair.md) adjusts how it is
measured and used.

## 4. Synthetic copies (2026-10-26)

On forgeries 0 to 2 of each authentic design-fold receipt (ADR-0021, development stream), the
pair lies on one of the copies of 102 of the 111 forgeries with a copy pasted as a rectangle and
neither scaled nor rotated, and of 10 of the 140 with only other copies. With a single copy per
forgery: 23 of 27 rectangles pasted as they were, 3 of 27 scaled or rotated, 3 of 27 ink-only
copies, 0 of 30 ink-only copies scaled or rotated.

- **Found while measuring.** A first count accepted a pair on a copy's source as finding that
  copy. It found about half of the ink-only and resampled copies, because copies on one
  forgery often share a source character. The report counts a pair on the pasted copy itself.

## 5. What copy-move does not find

- Copies pasted as their ink alone, or scaled or rotated: their blocks differ from the
  source's. The synthetic engine makes half of its copies one of these ways; the real forgers
  of the design fold pasted 12 of 14 so that the pair finds them.
- Copies of blank paper (X51007339158) and copies that are not exact (X51006620181, 2 % of
  its ink pixels equal).
- More than one copy per receipt: the detector reports the largest pair.
- Chance pairs on the JPEG grid are not removed: the three highest authentic scores of the
  chosen detector (at most 0.242) come from pairs at offsets that are multiples of 8 px. Leaving
  such offsets out would also drop a real copy pasted on the grid, one in 64 by chance.
- Re-encoding as JPEG destroys exactness; track R of Phase 7 will show how much survives.

## 6. Conventions no data chose

The ink contrast of 60 grey levels (taken from the feasibility check), the unsaturated share
of a quarter, the limit of 16 positions per hash, eight-connected groups and the two hash
constants.

## 7. Found in review, after the code was merged (2026-10-26)

A review of the merged code, before any receipt of the clean folds was scored, found two
defects. Both are fixed as ADR-0014 (decision 3) allows, with both results stated.

- **A match rested on the hash alone.** Equal blocks have equal hashes, but a polynomial hash
  with fixed bases is not random, so two different blocks could share one. Every match is now
  compared pixel by pixel, and a test forces a collision to show that it no longer counts. On
  the design fold every match was an exact duplicate before the fix, so no number changed.
- **The benign-mark baseline did not follow ADR-0030.** It combined the two regions' baselines
  as if their random boxes were independent, but drew both from the same random centres. The
  baseline now places one box per region independently
  (`docfraud.evaluation.localisation.any_box_baseline`, which the clean measurement uses too).
  The mean baseline of the flagged authentic receipts with a pair moves from 13.7 % to 13.9 %,
  and of the forged ones from 11.0 % to 10.4 %; nothing else in the report changes.

## Copy-move is frozen

With this record merged, copy-move is frozen: no further variant is tried on the design fold,
and later changes are limited to defects (ADR-0014, decision 3), each reported with both
results. Its single measurement on the clean folds follows, by the rules of ADR-0029
(section 4) as ADR-0030 amends them.
