Study: estimated outside the annotated rectangles on 4,000 sampled block positions, as in the JPEG trace comparison. System 1: alignments scored on every block and nothing left out, as it must on a receipt it has not seen. Step agreement: the share of coefficients used by either estimate whose steps are equal.
