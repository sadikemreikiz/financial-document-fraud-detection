# System 1 development

The development choices for system 1, the JPEG-lattice map of
[ADR-0016](decisions/0016-system-1-jpeg-trace.md), made on the design fold before its code is
frozen. The evidence is regenerated by
[`experiments/system1_development.py`](../experiments/system1_development.py) in
[system-1-development.md](../reports/forensics/system-1-development.md). Numbers on the design
fold are optimistic; system 1 is measured once on the clean folds.

## 1. The map (2026-10-13)

- Each block of the receipt's 8 x 8 grid gets the **suspicion one minus its lattice fit**, between
  0 and 2. Blocks that cannot be measured (a pixel at 0 or 255), pixels outside the block grid
  and receipts without a detectable lattice get 0, meaning no evidence. A receipt without
  evidence has a flat map, whose top region is the whole page and earns nothing against the
  random baseline ([ADR-0015](decisions/0015-pixel-measurement-rules.md)).
- System 1 sees no annotation: it estimates the lattice from every block and scores the 64
  alignments on every block, not on a random sample
  ([`lattice_map.py`](../src/docfraud/forensics/lattice_map.py)).
- **Evidence.** Without annotations the estimate matches the comparison study: the same
  alignment on all 60 receipts where both find a lattice, and equal steps on at least 94.6 % of
  the coefficients either uses. On the forged receipts with a lattice, the top region of the map
  hits an edit on 11 of 15, where a random box of the same size on the receipt's text hits
  2.0 % of the time on average.
- **Considered and not chosen.**
  - The mean fit over 3 x 3 blocks: its top region hits on 10 of 15 and it adds a window size.
  - The grid agreement where no lattice is found: it would have served two forged receipts
    (X51005719823 and X51008099071) whose lattice the sampled scoring of the comparison study
    missed. System 1 finds the lattice on both, and its top region hits on both. A fallback
    would add a second measurement with its own window and scale.

### Found while coding the map: alignments are scored on every block

The comparison study scored the 64 alignments on 4,000 sampled block positions. On
X51008099071, two samples gave different answers (a best score of 0.61 without a lattice, 0.96
with one); across the design fold this happened on 1 of 113 receipts. A system whose answer
depends on a random draw is not acceptable, so system 1 scores every block.

- This also finds a lattice on **73 of the 113** design-fold receipts instead of 60 (15 of the
  19 forged instead of 13): on receipts with much paper clipped to white, a sample held too few
  measurable blocks.
- All 13 additional lattices start at alignment (0, 0), where the grid of an uncropped JPEG
  starts, and on the 6 of them with a detectable grid the two agree.
- The margins of the scores over every block still leave the threshold of 0.25 in an empty gap,
  between 0.146 and 0.328.
- A receipt takes 1.4 s (median; at most 5.6 s) on the reference machine.
- The comparison study and its report keep the sampled scoring with which they were measured
  (`receipt_lattice` samples when it is given a generator).
