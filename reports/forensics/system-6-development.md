Size check: the Spearman rank correlation of document score and pixel count with its 95 % interval (bootstrap over receipts, 10,000 resamples, seed 0), as ADR-0015 (section 6) fixes it; it passes if the interval includes zero on the receipts with evidence (ADR-0017). The first score that passes is chosen, whatever its average precision. Design fold: optimistic.
